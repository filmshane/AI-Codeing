## competitor_other_offers

### competitor-001 — I'm waiting because I'm not sure I want to sell at all.

That's okay — clarity beats a bad yes. If you don't sell, what will you do next: hold, renovate, or list? If holding costs or the condition are stressful, a cash as-is sale is one tool, not the only path. I'm happy to leave you with a written offer range and let you sit with it. What would you need to see to feel comfortable either way?

**Talking points:**
- Permission not to sell
- Ask plan B
- Leave written range

**Do not say:**
- Force a decision today

**Sources:**
- YouTube `LugeMAjzK10` — Watch Me Call Seller Back To Make CASH OFFER — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LugeMAjzK10

### web-ddgs-comp-agent-said-more-001 — Another agent said they can get me more money.

**Talking points:**
